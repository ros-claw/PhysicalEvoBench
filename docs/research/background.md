# Research scope

Isaac Lab-Arena supplies composable physical tasks and policy evaluation infrastructure.
PhysicalEvoBench proposes a separate auditable skill-adaptation protocol on top of it; Arena
is not itself evidence that our self-improvement loop has run.

The implementation specification also names EmbodiedCLBench, EmbodiedEvo, LIBERO and
RoboDojo. Their specific claims and cited identifiers have not yet been independently
reviewed for this repository. No priority or novelty claim is made here.

Project-measured evidence currently consists of environment inspection, dependency and
checkpoint provenance, and CPU contract checks. Physical adaptation results are pending.
